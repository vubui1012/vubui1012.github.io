Privacy Policy - Video Studio Uploader

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Video Studio Uploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent (Google OAuth), the app uses the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl to upload videos, set thumbnails and captions, manage playlists and read basic channel and video statistics of the owner's channels.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not collect data from any other users.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the YouTube Terms of Service.
